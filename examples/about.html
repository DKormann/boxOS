BOXOS

A tiny backend made of transparent, permanent functions.

Submit restricted JavaScript once. BOXOS stores it under the SHA-256 hash of its exact source, gives it persistent state, and lets anyone inspect what it does.

Reducers update isolated state transactionally. Procedures make network requests and compose reducers. Immutable HTML pages receive permanent addresses and their own browser origins.

Permissions are effortless: apps ask for exactly what they need, people approve with their account, and BOXOS securely scopes that approval to the intended function. No API keys, auth service, or identity plumbing to build.
